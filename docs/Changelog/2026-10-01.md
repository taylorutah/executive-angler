# 2026-10-01 — Fly box inventory export

CSV and PDF export for fly-box inventory on the website. From `/flybox` or a box detail page, an angler picks one box, several boxes, or all, then downloads a reorder CSV or a printable PDF. Numbers come from live `fly_box_entries_v3` (tied / bought / target, plus Total and Need) — not the stale `listVariantsInBox` helper. No Pro gate. No iOS in this pass.
