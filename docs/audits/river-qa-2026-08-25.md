`no_fly` insects that are real hatches, not chart junk: Hendrickson (20 rivers), Light Cahill (19), Hexagenia / Hex variants (5+), Early Black Stonefly (3), Grey Drake (2), Blue Quill, Little Yellow Stonefly, Sulphur Spinner, Olive Dun, Daddy Longlegs, Mysis Shrimp. The rest of the 33 are `not_hatch` labels (Runoff, Streamers, Spey, Sockeye run, Poppers).

`src/lib/hatch-flies.ts` has no static insect→pattern table. Matching is name / `imitates` overlap. Audited; no mapping was clearly pointing at the wrong pattern. Gaps are missing catalog names (Hendrickson, Light Cahill), not a bad alias. No patterns were invented.

Window flags after dropping cases that are not sure: **Hendrickson@September** on `ausable-river-new-york` only. April salmonfly on Hat Creek / Upper Sac was not flagged — that timing is used on those waters and was not something this lane would rewrite.
