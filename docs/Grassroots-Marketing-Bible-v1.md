## 2. Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Who you are in every post:** A serious fly angler who built the tool he wished existed. Not a marketer. Not a brand. A guy who fishes hard, ties his own flies, and got tired of scribbling in a notebook.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Share real conditions, real patterns, real data | Use corporate phrases ("we're excited to announce") |
| Speak like you're texting a fishing buddy | Use emojis excessively (one per post max, if any) |
| Reference specific rivers, hatches, fly sizes | Be generic ("great day on the water!") |
| Let the tool speak through examples | Hard-sell features with bullet lists |
| Use present tense, active voice | Over-explain or apologize |

**Voice examples:**
- Yes: "BWOs came off around 2pm on the Madison yesterday. Size 18. The guys throwing 16s were getting refusals. Logged it — same pattern as last April."
- No: "We're thrilled to launch our new fly fishing journal app! Sign up today for free!"

## 3. Content Pillars & Post Formulas

### Pillar 1: SESSION INTEL (Journal)
**Theme:** Show what session logging looks like and why it matters.

**Formulas:**
- **The Replay** — "Here's what my last session looked like in Executive Angler: [screenshot of session detail]. 14 fish, all on size 18 Pheasant Tails below the riffle. Water at 46°F, 1,200 cfs. Two years of logging and I can tell you — that's the sweet spot on this stretch."
- **The Pattern** — "Logged 40 sessions on the Green River over two seasons. The data says: 52°F water temp + 800-1,100 cfs + overcast = 3x catch rate on dries. You can't see that in one trip. You see it in forty."
- **The Comparison** — "April 2025 vs April 2026, same river, same flies. What changed? [screenshot of year-over-year stats]"

### Pillar 2: FLY RECIPES (Flies)
**Theme:** Share specific fly patterns with tying details — the kind of content fly tyers screenshot and save.

**Formulas:**
- **The Recipe Card** — "Parachute Adams — the fly that belongs in every box. [screenshot of recipe from workbench]. Hook: TMC 100 #14. Thread: Uni 8/0 gray. Wing: white calf body hair. Hackle: grizzly and brown mixed. Full recipe + materials list on Executive Angler."
- **The Matchup** — "If BWOs are popping on your home water right now, here are 3 patterns that cover the hatch: [name them]. All in the library with full tying recipes."
- **The Workbench Peek** — "Built this Copper John variant in the workbench last night. Substituted tungsten bead for brass. Materials list auto-updates. [screenshot]"

### Pillar 3: RIVER CONDITIONS (Rivers)
**Theme:** Timely, useful intel that makes anglers check Executive Angler before they check the weather.

**Formulas:**
- **The Report** — "Madison River right now: 1,450 cfs at Kirby Ranch, 44°F. Midges in the morning, BWOs around 2pm. Runoff hasn't hit yet but it's coming. [screenshot of river page]. Updated every 15 minutes."
- **The Alert** — "Green River dropped below 1,000 cfs overnight. If you've been waiting for wade-friendly flows — this is your window."
- **The Hatch Intel** — "April hatch chart for [river]: [screenshot]. Skwalas winding down, Mother's Day caddis building. The chart shows what to expect week by week."

### Pillar 4: COMMUNITY PROOF (Feed / Social)
**Theme:** Show that real anglers are using this — activity, catches, engagement.

**Formulas:**
- **The Feed Peek** — "This showed up in my Executive Angler feed this morning. [screenshot of community catch]. Brown trout, 19 inches, CDC emerger, Yellowstone."
- **The Milestone** — "[X] sessions logged this week across [Y] rivers. The community is building."
- **The Question** — "What's your home water fishing like right now? Drop it below — I'll pull up the live conditions on Executive Angler."

### Pillar 5: APP TEASER (iOS / Android)
**Theme:** Build anticipation without overselling. Web is live and fully functional.

**Formulas:**
- **The Sneak Peek** — "Everything you see on executiveangler.com is coming to your pocket. iOS and Android — soon. Web app live now."
- **The Mobile Moment** — "Imagine logging a session streamside in 30 seconds. That's what we're building. Until then — web app works great on mobile."

## 7. iOS & Android Teaser Strategy

**Principle:** The web app IS the product right now. Tease mobile as "the same intelligence in your pocket" — never make anyone feel like they should wait for the app.

**Week 1-2:** Subtle footer mention only: "Web app live now. iOS & Android coming soon."
**Week 3:** One dedicated teaser post per platform: "Everything on Executive Angler is coming to your pocket. Web app works great on mobile right now."
**Week 4:** If iOS is approved, announce with a dedicated post. If not, another teaser with: "iOS beta testing now. Web app live and free."
**Ongoing:** Every 4th-5th post includes "iOS & Android coming soon" as the last line — never the lead.

**Do NOT:**
- Show iOS screenshots until the app is approved
- Promise a date unless you have one
- Make the web app feel like a placeholder

## 9. Engagement & Community-Building Tactics

### Daily (5-10 minutes total, while posting)

1. **Comment on 3-5 fly fishing posts** from accounts with 1K-50K followers. Leave genuinely useful comments — specific, knowledgeable, not "great catch!" but "That looks like a size 16 caddis emerger — were they taking it in the film or subsurface?" Your profile does the selling.

2. **Reply to 2-3 tweets** in fly fishing conversations on X. Add value. Link only when directly relevant ("I actually track hatch charts for that river — here's the current one: [link]").

### Weekly (15-20 minutes, once per week)

3. **Post in 2-3 Facebook groups** — not promotional posts. Answer questions, share tips. When someone asks about a specific river or fly, share a link naturally: "I put together a full hatch chart for that river here: [link]." Groups to target:
   - The Fly Fishing Group (506771179445716)
   - The Fly Fishing Community
   - Brown Trout Nation
   - State-specific groups (Montana Fly Fishing, Utah Fly Fishing, etc.)

4. **Respond to every comment and DM** on your posts within 24 hours. Early accounts live or die on reply rate.

### Bi-weekly

5. **Search X for relevant keywords** and reply helpfully:
   - "fly fishing journal" / "fishing log" / "catch tracking"
   - "hatch chart" + river names
   - "fly tying recipe" / "fly pattern"
   - "USGS flow" + fishing context

### What NOT to do
- Don't DM people unprompted to try the app
- Don't post your link in other people's comment sections
- Don't post more than once per week in any Facebook group
- Don't follow/unfollow for growth — it's 2026, algorithms punish this

## 13. Risk Mitigation & Compliance

### CAN-SPAM / Anti-Spam
- Never send unsolicited DMs promoting the app
- Never scrape emails from social for marketing
- All email communication goes through Resend with proper unsubscribe
- If someone DMs asking about the app, respond helpfully — that's not spam

### Platform Compliance
- No engagement pods, follow/unfollow, or automation tools
- No fake accounts or sock puppets
- No purchasing followers or engagement
- Disclose if any content is AI-generated (your screenshots and data are real — you're fine)

### Brand Protection
- Never disparage competitors (ANGLR, IdentaFly, FlyFishFinder, etc.)
- If asked about competitors: "They're solid apps. We focused on combining the journal, fly library, and river intel in one place."
